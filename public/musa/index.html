The 5 rules that protect your money

  1. Ask for the OEP licence number and BEOE permission number — check them on beoe.gov.pk yourself.
  2. Contract first, payment second. Never the other way round.
  3. Pay companies, not personal accounts — and get an official receipt every time.
  4. No one can guarantee a visa. Only the immigration authority decides.
  5. Never travel on a visit visa to work. It leads to deportation and bans.
